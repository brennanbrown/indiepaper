Welcome to Indiepaper, a Hugo theme that embodies the principles of the smolweb and IndieWeb movements. This post demonstrates the various features and typographic elements available in the theme.

## Typography

The theme uses system fonts to ensure fast loading and consistent appearance across devices. Headers use a geometric humanist sans-serif stack, while body text uses an old-style serif for optimal readability.

## IndieWeb Features

This theme supports all major IndieWeb protocols:

- **Microformats2**: Every post includes h-entry markup
- **Webmentions**: Receive and display responses from across the web
- **Micropub**: Publish to your site from compatible clients
- **Rel-me**: Verify your identity on social platforms

## Accessibility

Indiepaper is built with accessibility in mind:

- Semantic HTML5 structure
- Proper heading hierarchy
- Keyboard navigation support
- Screen reader friendly
- 4.5:1 contrast ratios for text

This is just a sample of what's possible with Indiepaper. The theme stays out of your way so you can focus on what matters most: your content.
